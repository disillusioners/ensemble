## Objective

Own the four **externally-observable** surfaces of the feature — the configuration
knobs an operator turns (including their **root-`Config` wiring**), the observability
surfaces an operator reads, the **durable event's emitter and its wiring**, and the
**fence re-run** that proves the feature never touched the T4 seam. This phase writes
**no enforcement logic**; the envelope producer and the event **hook** live in
**Phase 2** (`ram_guard/errors.py`, `ram_guard/guard.py`).

**Phase ownership (arch §7, AMENDED — approver blocker 3), verbatim:**

> Phase 2 owns the event HOOK (the emitter injection, the WATCHING→KILLED call site, build_ram_event_payload) and the DQ6 consumer-gate artifact (pins-only).
> Phase 5 owns the event EMITTER (EventKind.RAM_LIMIT_EXCEEDED, daemon/services/ram_guard_events.py, the non-blocking loop bridge, the lifespan wiring) and the root-Config wiring of RamGuardConfig.

## Exit Criterion

- The config class loads with no env set, honours every `RAM_GUARD_*` knob at its
  **ruled** default and bound, keeps the yaml block comment-only, and the re-arm
  constants are **absent** from the field set.
- The **root `Config` wiring** is live: `config.ram_guard.*` is reachable from
  `load_config()`, the env tier binds, and the out-of-range rung **aborts boot**
  (asserted via `pytest.raises(ValidationError)` — **no untestable disclosure remains**).
- The master switch `ENSEMBLE_RAM_GUARD` implements the reaper semantics and OFF is
  a **complete no-op** (no thread, no registration, no measurement).
- The ceiling is **runtime-injectable per call** as an internal seam; the
  LLM-facing tool schemas are **unchanged** (asserted).
- The **event emitter** is implemented outside the package, wired at the composition
  root, and its bridge is **non-blocking** and uses `manager._get_event_loop()` —
  **not** `MainLoopBridge`.
- A drop is **visible**: `ram_events_dropped++` + the transition log line.
- Observability: exactly one event + the counter increments + a `ram-guard:`-prefixed
  log line per breach, with **zero** secret material and **zero** argv anywhere.
- `GET /api/resources/ram-guard` exists, is additive, carries the counters + the
  heartbeat, and is **not** referenced by `livez`/`readyz`.
- `tests/test_resource_control_seam.py` passes **unmodified**;
  `/control-capabilities` is byte-identical.
- The **withdrawn §6 consumer-count claim appears nowhere** in the plan set.
