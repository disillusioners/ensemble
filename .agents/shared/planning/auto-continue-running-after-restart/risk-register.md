## Top-3 watchlist for the implementation lane

1. **R1** — the AC4 property is the reason this design is safe; do not "simplify" the ordering or add a reaper.
2. **R2** — copy the three-layer envelope verbatim; do not invent a fourth pattern.
3. **R5/R8** — touch nothing in `claim_pending_task` or the terminal-token set; full-dir + cascade gates are the tripwires.
