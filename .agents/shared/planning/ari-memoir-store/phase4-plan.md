## 1. Objective

The privileged category `memoir` (#18, ARI-EXACTLY): `daemon/tools/memoir.py` with the five blessed tools behind the **auth-first** two-layer gate, the frozen refusal vocabulary, the adjudicated result shapes, and the kill-switch; the three-step registration seam completed (decorator-only is silently invisible); the ari allow entry + doctrine doc; the ONE-commit pin ceremony with a **grep-derived** pin set, a corrected re-stamp scope, and the FC9 prompt-budget measurement.
