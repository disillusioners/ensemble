## 1. Objective

Ship the **fast-by-default + reliable promote** behavior as one PR. Adaptive soak defaults to 60–120 s with early-exit on 3 consecutive green probes at 15 s cadence; total wall-clock budget of 900 s (15 min) at the top of `promote.sh` enforces per-phase deadlines with breach routing to the existing halt machinery; transient probe classification + bounded retries + boot-window grace close the v0.20.0 1.5 s transient-window race; deferred DELETE (audit-after-soak sequencing) moves the env-poison purge off the boot critical path while preserving every ordering guarantee. All four decisions ship together because their interactions are inseparable (decisions.md sequencing table line 528 — adaptive soak's stable gate is the dependency for audit-after-soak sequencing; transient handling feeds the probe classifier that adaptive soak consumes; total budget supplies the `ceremony_remaining_s` cap).
