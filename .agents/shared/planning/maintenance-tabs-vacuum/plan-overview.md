## Objective

Two coupled outcomes for the maintenance console at `/maintenance/checkpoint-cleanup`:

1. **Part 1 — FE wire-up.** Convert the single-section maintenance page into a Material `mat-tab-group` page with the existing Checkpoint wizard mounted in Tab 1 (AS-IS, byte-for-byte) and one disabled "Database" placeholder Tab 2, per the approved frozen design spec. Sticky tab bar; lazy-mount; section registry remains load-bearing; one-line extensibility preserved.
2. **Part 2 — Checkpoint cleanup VACUUM FULL option.** Add an operator-facing checkbox to the wizard's confirm step (default-ticked for interactive runs) that, when checked, escalates the **post-destroy state** (i.e. after the destructive prune's pair-excess blob deletion completes; the existing scheduled `VacuumMaintenanceJob` continues to do plain `VACUUM (ANALYZE)` only, per its structurally-locked module docstring at `daemon/services/vacuum_maintenance.py:36`) from plain `VACUUM (ANALYZE)` to `VACUUM FULL` on the three affected tables (`checkpoints`, `checkpoint_writes`, `checkpoint_blobs`). **No VACUUM step exists in the MANUAL lane today** (the manual `run_checkpoint_prunes` does destructive-prune-only; the plain-VACUUM work lives ONLY in the separate scheduled `VacuumMaintenanceJob` per N4); the new `_run_vacuum_full_after_cleanup` method is the FIRST VACUUM step in the manual lane. **Structural guard (4 base layers + 4 additional pin mechanics, Plan Review 2026-10-12 F1, hardened by approver 2026-10-12 B4):** the daily/scheduled BE path is made STRUCTURALLY INCAPABLE of escalating to `VACUUM FULL` regardless of payload, config, or future wrapper methods / subclass overrides. The 4 base layers (manual-only method, explicit kwarg, body pin, definition-site pin) are hardened by (i) intra-class transitive closure walk, (ii) subclass scan, (iii) call-site enumeration, (iv) one behavioral test under poisoned env/config. See `decisions.md §D-4` for the full 4-layer + 4-mechanic design.

The combined outcome (when both parts are live): an operator who clicks through the maintenance console can reclaim disk space via VACUUM FULL in a single interactive session, while the daily scheduled cleanup continues to use plain VACUUM (ANALYZE) — never FULL — with the structural impossibility enforced by 4 base AST-level test pins + 4 additional pin mechanics (closure walk, subclass scan, call-site enumeration, behavioral test), not by configuration.
