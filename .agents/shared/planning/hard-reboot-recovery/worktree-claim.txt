re-imported the package. SQLAlchemy's declarative registry
(SQLModel.metadata) is PROCESS-GLOBAL and survives the sys.modules

defined for this MetaData instance`. The armed gate destroyed the very
suite it was meant to protect.

`daemon` NON-EXECUTINGLY (`importlib.util.find_spec("daemon").origin`
— no pop, no re-import), with a PINNED test

no-sys.modules-mutation regression.
