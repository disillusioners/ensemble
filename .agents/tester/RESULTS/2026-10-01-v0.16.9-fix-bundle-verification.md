**Root cause** (bash -x trace + live process-tree + soak): fix #4's `_js_run_bounded` watcher `( sleep N; kill -KILL $pid ) &` — when the watched child wins the race, the watcher subshell is SIGKILLed but its `sleep N` is orphaned (ppid=1) and **holds the command-substitution pipe** → ~N s stall per bounded call even when the command completes instantly. Fix #4 routes all sweep calls (cat/date/kill -0) through the wrapper → ~25–45 s per sweep → suite 10 s → 652 s; the 300 s gate starves mid-§8l (`_js_run "$JS_L"` → `launcher.sh::_journal_sweep`).

**Impact characterization:** correctness intact — the sweep-hang fix achieves its goal (c5 real sweep at HEAD completed rc 0; soak completed; nothing hangs forever). Steady-state boots are unaffected (drill scenarios with no open txn ran sub-second — the sweep only does work when a stale txn exists). The regression lands on **recovery paths**: every stale-txn sweep now costs tens of seconds instead of ~instant — exactly the windows (post-halt boot, promote preflight `adopt_stale_txn`, launcher boot sweep after a crashed promote) when latency matters most.

**Recommendation (decision for council — tree frozen, no fix applied by tester):** ride a fix into v0.16.9 before promote: detach the watcher's sleep from the substitution pipe (redirect watcher stdio) or kill the watcher's process group on child success. ~1–3 lines in `_js_run_bounded`. Alternative: accept bounded-but-slow recovery sweeps and ticket for v0.16.10.
